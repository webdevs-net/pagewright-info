# Pagewright brand

*Draft v0.1, 2026-11-20. Pagewright is an internal WebDevs product, so this is deliberately
light: enough to keep the website, case study, README and posts consistent. The visual side
is in [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).*

## In one line

**Update your website by just asking, with powerful guardrails.**

Pagewright lets a business owner change their website by asking their own Claude or ChatGPT.
Every change lands on a private staging copy first; the owner looks, says "push it live", and
any earlier live version is one sentence away. It's the editing system behind the custom
sites WebDevs builds.

## Who it's for

Business owners (builders, contractors, studios, local services) who want to change their
own site's words, photos, colors and fonts without learning new software or waiting on a
developer. If you can type a request to your AI, you can edit your site.

## What we promise (and can prove)

| Promise | What makes it true |
|---|---|
| Just ask | A connector in the owner's own Claude or ChatGPT; plain-language requests |
| Nothing goes live by accident | Every edit goes to staging (bannered, hidden from search) until the owner says "push it live" |
| Undo anything | The last 100 live versions are kept; any one comes back with a sentence |
| The AI can't break the site | It only uses checked editing tools, which make the changes the site is built to accept; it never touches the code |
| It stays on-brand | Layout changes stay inside the limits the designer set; color contrast is checked |
| No developer for everyday updates | Words, photos, lists, colors, fonts, logo, footer, SEO and form email settings are all self-serve |

**Rules for claims:**
- Every claim matches the code and the README.
- No client counts, speed numbers or quotes unless they're real and approved.
- Don't say Pagewright is for sale, licensed or available to other studios: that's undecided.
- "Done" always pairs with "previewed first" somewhere nearby, so nobody reads it as "instantly live".
- "Anything" overstates it: Pagewright changes what the site is built to accept.

## Voice

- **Warm, plain, a little playful.** Short sentences. Talk like a helpful person, not a platform.
  This is the friendly sibling of WebDevs' more technical voice.
- **Benefit first, mechanism second.** "Change your hours before lunch", then "every change is
  previewed first".
- **No jargon** in owner-facing copy: no MCP, deploy, CMS, repo or staging environment. Say "preview",
  "go live", "bring back an earlier version". "Staging" is OK once it's been explained.
- **Name the AI as "your AI"**, or "Claude or ChatGPT" when it matters. Never imply we are, or are
  endorsed by, Anthropic or OpenAI.

## Visual direction, in brief

- **The hero visual is always a conversation next to the website:** the owner's request,
  the AI's reply with a preview link, and the page showing the change under a staging banner.
- **Illustrations, not fake screenshots.** Chat visuals use Pagewright's own neutral chat
  style, without Claude or ChatGPT logos or app chrome, and are labelled "Illustration".
  Real screenshots come only from an approved demo site.
- **Example businesses are fictional** ("Hillside Builders", "Bayside Bakery") unless a client
  has agreed to be shown.

## Name

**Pagewright**: one word, capital P. A wright is a maker (playwright, shipwright), so it means
the maker of your pages. Don't write PageWright, Page Wright or pagewright.io.
