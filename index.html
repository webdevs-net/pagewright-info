Questions

Good questions, honest answers.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can type or speak a request to Claude or ChatGPT, you can update your site. Talking is often quicker: the Claude and ChatGPT apps take voice input, and Loquio, our free voice dictation app (coming soon), lets you speak a request into any app. We handle all the setup, and you never see any code.

Which AI assistants does it work with?

Claude and ChatGPT. You use your own account, the same one you already chat with.

What if I don't like a change?

If it's still in the preview, just ask for it to be thrown away. If it's already live, ask to go back to an earlier version. Your last 100 published versions are kept.

Can the AI wreck my site?

No. It can only make the kinds of changes your site was built to accept, and anything that would break a page is refused. Nothing reaches your visitors until you approve it.

How fast do changes show up?

Right away. The preview updates as soon as the change is made, and your live site updates the moment you say so.

Can more than one person on my team make changes?

Yes. Anyone you share the editor password with can connect their own Claude or ChatGPT.

What if I'd still rather have someone do it for me?

That's fine. Pagewright is there when you want it. You can always reach out to us instead.
